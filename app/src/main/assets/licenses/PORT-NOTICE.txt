Seanime TV Port
Unofficial Android TV adaptation by Malina.
Copyright (c) 2026 Malina, for contributions to this port.

Based on Seanime and Seanime Denshi by 5rahim and contributors:
https://github.com/5rahim/seanime
https://github.com/5rahim/seanime/tree/main/seanime-denshi

This port is a separate native Kotlin/Jetpack Compose implementation for Android TV.
It adapts the experience to TV remote navigation and uses on-device provider plugins,
AniList integration, Media3 playback, and Android TV home-screen integration.
These port-specific changes are not an official upstream release.
